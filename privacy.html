Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Channel Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the connected YouTube channels. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their details and read basic channel and video statistics for those channels.

The tool stores OAuth tokens only on the owner's own computer. It does not collect, sell or share data about any other person, and it does not access any data beyond the connected channels.

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
